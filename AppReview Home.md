What every review checks

## Four questions before any verdict.

The same checklist runs on every app, whether it's a wallet, a course or a rent finder.

### Does it work in real use?

We install it and use it the way you would, not just read the store page.

### What does the fine print say?

Terms, refund rules and permissions, compared with what the ads claim.

### Can you get your money out?

Payments, withdrawals and support, tested where the app handles money.

### Is it still true today?

Apps change. We update a review when the app or its terms change.
